## Headline

The EESC **welcomes** CADA's objective of strengthening Europe's cloud and AI ecosystem and reducing
dependence on non-European providers, **but requires targeted revisions** — on building European
data-centre capacity and steering public procurement toward European providers — and adds a distinct
social, environmental and financing overlay to the debate.

## Recommendations (article-mapped)

- **Financing** — the proposal mandates outcomes it cannot guarantee it will finance. Treat cloud and
  AI infrastructure as a **protected priority in the next MFF**, with resources matching the capacity
  objectives; publish a **consolidated overview of available EU financing before the [Art 7](../../extracts/commission/COM-2026-502_leadership-initiatives-art3-9.md#article-7) national-strategy deadline**;
  and have the Commission + EIB mobilise institutional/private capital (pension funds, insurers)
  toward data-centre and cloud infrastructure as a recognised investment class.
- **Social consultation ([Art 7](../../extracts/commission/COM-2026-502_leadership-initiatives-art3-9.md#article-7))** — Member States must **meaningfully consult social partners, civil
  society and consumer organisations** when developing or substantially revising national cloud/AI
  strategies (the proposal references neither social dialogue/just transition nor consumer bodies).
- **Acceleration zones ([Art 10](../../extracts/commission/COM-2026-502_data-centres-art10-15.md#article-10))** — make designation **conditional on a social and environmental impact
  assessment** (health, employment, energy and water demand); add a defined, time-limited **public
  consultation** period; invite Member States to set up a **compensation mechanism** for affected
  local communities and to mitigate health risks.
- **Power & connectivity** — a **strict 6-month** obligation on network operators to indicate available
  connection capacity; faster connections are no substitute for generation, so the Commission should
  assess whether **EU electricity generation can keep pace** with tripling data-centre capacity.
- **Procurement** — give the **"Union added value" award criterion ([Art 32](../../extracts/commission/COM-2026-502_public-sector-use-art29-33.md#article-32))** significant weight;
  develop a **European sustainable cloud/AI certification** to complement the [Art 11](../../extracts/commission/COM-2026-502_data-centres-art10-15.md#article-11) sustainability
  indicators; turn the **indicative 25% SME objective ([Art 33](../../extracts/commission/COM-2026-502_public-sector-use-art29-33.md#article-33)) into a shared requirement** with concrete
  implementation incentives.
- **Skills** — **co-design** the cloud/AI skills curriculum with social partners (building on the
  Centres for AI), supporting continuous learning and STEM, with **targeted measures for young people**
  (absent from the proposal).
- **Capability erosion (monitoring, [Art 29](../../extracts/commission/COM-2026-502_public-sector-use-art29-33.md#article-29))** — flags a risk not in the monitoring framework: the
  gradual, silent **erosion of human and institutional capability** to operate/oversee (and work
  without) the deployed systems; recommends the monitoring obligations, national strategies and
  public-sector risk assessments cover the **"absorption side" of adoption**.
