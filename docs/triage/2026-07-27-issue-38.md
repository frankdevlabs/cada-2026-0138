## Assessment

**In-scope for 2026/0138 (CADA).** Confirmed by reading the committed fiche PDF (kst-1263575) and the
Beslisnota (blg-1263576):

- The fiche appraises the **umbrella Communication COM(2026) 503** ("European Tech Sovereignty",
  accompanied by an EU Open Source Strategy; received 3 Jun 2026) — the package-level document above CADA
  and CAII, **not** a `keep_apart` sibling. It expressly names **CADA** as the demand-side instrument
  "die de vraag naar Europese cloud-, AI- en digitale oplossingen versterkt" and requires the Communication
  + Open Source Strategy to **dovetail with CADA and CAII**.
- **Chapter 4 (Open Source Strategy)** maps directly onto **CADA Art 41 open-source-first**: the cabinet is
  positive on the strategy, endorses "open-source-first" procurement, the OSPO network and the EDIC Digital
  Commons (NL co-founder/chair), and will seek clarification on the open-source-first principle's scope.
- The fiche's **central critique** is CADA-shaped: the package's scope stays too narrow — CADA covers
  datacentre-capacity build-out + sovereign-cloud adoption and CAII the semiconductor chain, but no
  instrument explicitly develops **AI models/software**; the cabinet will press for additional EU action so
  CADA + CAII cover the whole AI stack. It **defers** its precise **Data Centre Acceleration Zones** position
  to the separate CADA fiche (nr. 4395).
