## What was flagged

Two newly tabled Tweede Kamer motions in the Telecomraad (TTE Council) dossier 21501-33, both naming
**CADA (2026/0138)** explicitly, tabled at the plenary debate following the Telecom Council.

Confirmed against the source RSS + the authentic motion PDFs (web version unavailable; PDFs read):

- **Kamerstuk 21501-33, nr. 1265** (= kst-1282100) — *"Motie van het lid Van den Berg over bij
  onderhandelingen over de CADA en de DNA bepleiten dat nieuwe verplichtingen aantoonbaar aanvullend
  zijn"* (sole indiener **D.J. van den Berg**; **voorgesteld 25 Nov 2026**, published 26 Nov; status
  "Onopgemaakt" = freshly tabled). Recital: the **CADA** and **Digital Networks Act (DNA)** proposals add
  new obligations alongside existing rules incl. **NIS2, the Cybersecurity Act and the AVG (GDPR)**, and
  overlap can create unnecessary admin burden esp. for SMEs. Operative clause verbatim: *"verzoekt de
  regering bij de onderhandelingen over de CADA en de DNA te bepleiten dat nieuwe verplichtingen
  aantoonbaar aanvullend zijn op bestaande regels, dat bedrijven bij overlappende meldplichten dezelfde
  gegevens waar mogelijk één keer kunnen aanleveren en dat de administratieve en financiële gevolgen voor
  het mkb worden meegewogen"* — i.e. asks the government, in the CADA/DNA negotiations, to argue that new
  obligations be **demonstrably additional** to existing rules, that firms with overlapping reporting
  duties can supply the same data once where possible, and that SME admin/financial impact be weighed.
  **Package-adjacent** — couples CADA with the DNA (a separate file). Status: **tabled, vote pending.**
- **Kamerstuk 21501-33, nr. 1264** (= kst-1282099) — *"Motie van het lid Van den Berg over borgen dat
  Nederland zelf beslist of en waar het datacenterversnellingszones aanwijst"* (sole indiener **D.J. van
  den Berg**; **voorgesteld 25 Nov 2026**, published 26 Nov; "Onopgemaakt"). Recital: CADA proposes a
  framework for **datacenter acceleration zones** (datacenterversnellingszones), and location/siting
  choices tie to national considerations on space, energy and water. Operative clause verbatim:
  *"verzoekt de regering in de Europese onderhandelingen te borgen dat Nederland zelf beslist óf en waar
  het datacenterversnellingszones aanwijst en welke nationale ruimtelijke en maatschappelijke voorwaarden
  daarbij gelden"* — i.e. asks the government to **safeguard that the Netherlands itself decides whether
  and where it designates data-centre acceleration zones**, and which national spatial/societal conditions
  apply. This bears directly on CADA's acceleration-zone mechanism. Status: **tabled, vote pending.**

The other five motions in the same 26/11 batch (stalkerware/mSpy, satellite-encryption keys, copper
phase-out, enforcement gaps, VLOP supervision) do not concern CADA — out of scope.

## Assessment

**In scope and directly CADA-relevant — but tabled national-parliament scrutiny, not a government
position.**

- *For scope:* unlike the 28 Sep 2026 datacentre-environment motions (#53), both motions **name CADA
  explicitly**; nr. 1264 is squarely on **CADA's data-centre acceleration-zone mechanism** (a core
  provision this repo tracks under `docs/provisions`/`docs/instruments`), and nr. 1265 bears on the
  CADA negotiation (burden/overlap with NIS2, CSA, GDPR). Both instruct the NL government's **EU
  negotiating line**, the layer `docs/member-state-positions.md` exists to track.
- *Weight / hedge:* these are **member motions tabled by a single member (Van den Berg), with the vote
  pending** — not adopted Kamer instructions and not the cabinet's own position (which is the BNC-fiche,
  nr. 4395). The acceleration-zone motion (nr. 1264) **reinforces the subsidiarity concern already in the
  CADA BNC-fiche** (which contested the acceleration-zone designation duty); nr. 1265 adds an
  anti-overlap / SME-burden angle, coupled with the DNA.
- *Not the `keep_apart` sibling* (Chips Act 2.0, 2026/0139): both motions concern datacentres/cloud and
  name CADA, not semiconductors.

Faithful integration therefore records both as **hedged national-parliament scrutiny**, cited by URL —
**no `data/documents.yaml` registration / committed PDF** (a single-member tabled motion is scrutiny,
not a government position document; register only if adopted and it becomes a standing instruction —
mirroring the #53 treatment). No per-provision CADA claim beyond the verbatim operative asks is made.
