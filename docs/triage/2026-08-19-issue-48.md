## Assessment

**Noise — already integrated. No new document; recommend closing as already-handled.**

- The "Summary of the Impact Assessment" is the **executive summary of the impact assessment**,
  which the repo already holds as **`SWD-2026-503`** — committed at
  `sources/commission/SWD-2026-503_impact-assessment-summary_2026-06-03.pdf` and registered in
  `data/documents.yaml` / `sources/README.md`.
- The library page labels it **"SWD(2026) 502 — Summary"**. This is the exact mislabeling the repo
  already anticipated: `data/documents.yaml` (`SWD-2026-502` note) records that *"the executive
  summary carries its own number, SWD(2026) 503 … secondary reporting often mislabels it SWD(2026)
  502 summary."* The EU numbering convention gives the IA report one number (502) and its executive
  summary the next (503); there is only one summary of this IA.
- Item 5 is **not new**: the earlier `T1-06` flag **#21** (closed 2026-07-01 as already-integrated)
  recorded a 2026-06-24 baseline whose download list *already* contained "SWD(2026) 502 — Summary of
  the Impact Assessment". The current 4→5 count change reflects the Annexes and Part 2 now being
  listed as separate rows, not a genuinely new companion document. The page's own "Last update
  8 June 2026" banner was not bumped, consistent with no new publication.
- In scope (CADA, 2026/0138) — but nothing to integrate.
